title: No Ticket Home
slot: Final / major boss theme
starter: boss_choir
voices: Announcer = the intro line | Verse lead = your voice, low | Chorus lead = your voice with Raspy, or Female lead | Backing = Choir on the chorus | Gang = Gang shout on "Not yours!"
hits: yes (some): on "Not yours!" and the last words of each chorus line
notes: Big, slow-to-say words over a fast orchestra: one syllable per beat in the chorus, so it feels huge. The choir sings the title on long open vowels ("no-o ti-cket ho-ome").
---
[Intro, spoken by the announcer]
This train does not stop here.

[Verse 1]
You sold the city a perfect sleep,
Wrote our names where the dark runs deep.
But every passenger knows the rule:
The last stop's ours, not yours to rule.

[Pre-Chorus]
Wake up, wake up,
The lights are coming on.
Stand up, stand up,
The long night's almost gone.

[Chorus]
No ticket home, no way back,
We tear the dark from the end of the track.
No ticket home, no one runs,
We are the many, you are the one!
(Not yours!)

[Bridge, slow, then building]
Every stop we lost,
Every name you stole,
Every door you closed,
We take it back, we take it all.

[Final Chorus]
No ticket home, no way back,
We tear the dark from the end of the track.
No ticket home, no one runs,
We are the many, you are the one!
(Not yours!)
