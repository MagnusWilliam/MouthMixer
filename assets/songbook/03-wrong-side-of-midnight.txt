title: Wrong Side of Midnight
slot: City night theme / hideout

voices: Whisper = the verses | Lead = Breathy on the chorus | hum a tenor-sax line as a Riff or Answer-line piece
hits: no
notes: Quiet and close. Whispered verses, a breathy two-line chorus. The title is the chorus's last words, held on the open "-night" vowel. Leave gaps: the sax answers each line (record an Answer line piece).

[Intro]

[Verse 1, whispered]
Neon on the wet stone,
Every window's someone's secret.
I walk the long way home,
Just to hear the city breathing.

[Chorus]
Stay, stay, we're not done yet,
On the wrong side of midnight.
Stay, stay, don't let go yet,
On the wrong side of midnight.

[Verse 2, whispered]
Last bus sighing at the stop,
Somebody laughing two floors up.
Streetlights humming out of key,
Nobody here is watching me.

[Chorus]
Stay, stay, we're not done yet,
On the wrong side of midnight.
Stay, stay, don't let go yet,
On the wrong side of midnight.

[Outro]
