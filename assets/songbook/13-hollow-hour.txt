title: Hollow Hour
slot: The hour between days: the Line 0 run (inspired by Burn My Dread)
starter: night_hiphop
voices: Verse lead = your voice, low and close | Chorus lead = your voice | Rap = spoken, on the beat
hits: none
notes: Dark trip-hop in F minor, 98: a piano loop, strings, a sub bass, scratches. The flat-II chord (Gb) is the dread colour the reference leans on. Sing the verses almost spoken; open up only in the chorus.
---
Hollow Hour

[Intro]

[Verse 1, Verse lead]
Every clock in the city stops at twelve,
and the silence has a sound all by itself.
Streetlights hum a note they never learned,
and the platform waits for those who don't return.

[Pre-Chorus, Verse lead]
Hold your breath, the doors are open wide,
nobody else can see the other side.

[Chorus, Chorus lead]
In the hollow hour I am wide awake,
every shadow knows the name I'd never take.
If the night is swallowing the light,
I will walk it, I will walk it to the end of the line.

[Rap, Rap]
Count the cars, one, two, three, the fourth is never lit,
tickets in my pocket with a date that doesn't fit.
Faces in the window but the seats are all empty,
I don't flinch, I don't run, let the dark come and tempt me.

[Verse 2, Verse lead]
There's a door that only opens when you're gone,
and a voice that knows the words to every song.
I could stay asleep and let it pass me by,
but I've seen too many friends forget to try.

[Pre-Chorus, Verse lead]
Hold your breath, the doors are open wide,
nobody else can see the other side.

[Chorus, Chorus lead]
In the hollow hour I am wide awake,
every shadow knows the name I'd never take.
If the night is swallowing the light,
I will walk it, I will walk it to the end of the line.

[Bridge, Verse lead]
When the morning comes, they won't remember,
only I will know how long it took.

[Final Chorus, Chorus lead]
In the hollow hour I am wide awake,
every shadow knows the name I'd never take.
If the night is swallowing the light,
I will walk it, I will walk it to the end of the line.

[Outro]
