Second Platform

[Intro]

[Verse 1, Verse lead]
Morning train, the same two coffees,
one for me and one for who?
Ticket stub inside my jacket,
written in a hand I almost knew.

[Pre-Chorus, Verse lead]
Every corner feels like I've been here before,
every echo knocks on a different door.

[Chorus, Chorus lead]
Same streets, different light,
I know your name but I can't place the night.
Keep on moving, keep it close,
the things you forget are the things that matter most.

[Verse 2, Verse lead]
Photo booth on the second platform,
four small frames and one is blank.
Someone laughing right beside me,
I turn around and there is no one there to thank.

[Pre-Chorus, Verse lead]
Every corner feels like I've been here before,
every echo knocks on a different door.

[Chorus, Chorus lead]
Same streets, different light,
I know your name but I can't place the night.
Keep on moving, keep it close,
the things you forget are the things that matter most.

[Bridge, Chorus lead]
If the city takes the names away,
I will write them on the windows every day.
Breathe on the glass and they come back,
one more letter on the morning track.

[Final Chorus, Chorus lead]
Same streets, different light,
I know your name and I'll find you by tonight.
Keep on moving, hold it close,
the things you forget are the things that matter most.
