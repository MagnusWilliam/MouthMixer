    Have lyrics? Set the song up from them

    Paste your lyrics with a header for each part, like [Verse 1], [Pre-Chorus], [Chorus], [Rap Verse], [Bridge, slow, then building]. Each header becomes a section with the right length and feel (the chorus lifts, the rap drops into a half-time pocket, the intro gets the flashy treatment). This replaces the song board below; your pieces stay. The words go with the song to the Sing screen.

    Song board

    Left to right is the song. Switch pieces on in the sections where they should play. Bridge sections use the bridge chords; your pieces follow them.

    Microphone

    Pick the mic MouthMixer listens to. The meter should jump when you hum.

    Beatbox calibration

    Do each sound about 10 times, with the pop shield where you normally keep it. MouthMixer learns your kick, snare and hats. Redo it if you move the shield.

    Mic test (keyboard vs voice)

    Type normally for 8 seconds, then hum for 8 seconds. Aim for at least 15 dB between them. If it's less, move the mic closer to your mouth, point its back at the keyboard, and lower the gain.

    Background listening

    Off by default: you record on purpose in Make. Turn it on to have MouthMixer catch hums and beatbox while you work (and the Ctrl+Alt+M hotkey).

    Lyrics with Claude

    Paste a Claude API key to have lyrics written for your songs (from console.anthropic.com). It's saved only on this computer. Without one you can still type your own words.
