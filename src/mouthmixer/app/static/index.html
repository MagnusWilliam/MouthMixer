    Add a part

    What sound are you going for? Suggestions are ranked by how well they pair with what's already playing and by the sound you pick. Each one says why.

    Words are fitted to your melody: one syllable per note, strong syllables on strong beats, open vowels ("ah", "oh") on long high notes, and a short hook you can chant.

    Write with Claude

    Describe what the song is about. A few words is enough; details help (a place, a feeling, a moment in the game).

    Or type your own words

    Microphone

    Pick the mic MouthMixer listens to. The meter should jump when you hum.

    Beatbox calibration

    Do each sound about 10 times, with the pop shield where you normally keep it. MouthMixer learns your kick, snare and hats. Redo it if you move the shield.

    Mic test (keyboard vs voice)

    Type normally for 8 seconds, then hum for 8 seconds. Aim for at least 15 dB between them. If it's less, move the mic closer to your mouth, point its back at the keyboard, and lower the gain.

    Lyrics with Claude

    Paste a Claude API key to have lyrics written for your songs (from console.anthropic.com). It's saved only on this computer. Without one you can still type your own words.

    Add a recording

    A WAV file of a hum, or a long recording of your workday (only the musical parts are kept).
