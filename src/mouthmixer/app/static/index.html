Beatbox calibration

Do each sound about 10 times, with the pop shield where you normally keep it. MouthMixer learns your kick, snare and hats. Redo it if you move the shield.

Mic test (keyboard vs voice)

Type normally for 8 seconds, then hum for 8 seconds. It shows how far apart they are; aim for at least 15 dB. If it's less, move the mic closer to your mouth, point its back at the keyboard, and lower the gain.
