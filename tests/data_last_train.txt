“LAST TRAIN, FIRST LIGHT”

[Intro, spoken over the station chime]
Doors are closing…
Mind the gap.

[Verse 1]
Midnight hum on an empty platform,
Clock says zero but my heart’s still running.
Paper ticket with a name unwritten,
Something in the dark says you’ve been chosen.

[Pre-Chorus]
Hold on tight, don’t look back,
City’s sleeping on a broken track.
Put the mask on, let it show
The part of me I didn’t know.

[Chorus]
Ride, ride, ride on the last train,
Every stop is a door with a different name.
We don’t fade, we don’t break,
We’re the passengers the night can’t take.
Ride, ride, till the morning shows,
Out on Line Zero, where nobody goes.
Hearts on the timetable, we won’t be late.
This is our line, so we hold the gate!

[Rap Verse]
Tap in, step up, lights flicker green,
Hollow eyes watching from the in-between.
Got my crew on the left, got my fear on the right,
Turn the static to a signal, turn the signal to light.
Six years gone but the rails remember,
Burn like a match in the cold December.
No more waiting for someone to save me.
I’m the one who rewrites the ending, baby.

[Bridge, slow, then building]
If I lose my way, call my name.
If the doors won’t open, we’ll break the frame.
The line goes dark…
(but we shine anyway)

[Final Chorus]
Ride, ride, ride on the last train…
(Next stop: tomorrow.)
